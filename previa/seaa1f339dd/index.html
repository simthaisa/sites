Meet the artist

Hi, I’m Alyne.

I’ve called myself an artist since I was a little girl, making crafts with my mom and grandma: embroidery, sewing, wood painting and jewelry.

Art became my calling in college. A wedding caricature painted on porcelain, a gift for a friend, turned into a studio full of orders.

Today I paint on porcelain, canvas and wood and design jewelry. Colorful, maximalist and a little out of the box, made to color the lives of the people you love.
