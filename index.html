Questions

Do I need to have texts and photos ready?+

No. I write the texts with you from your briefing. Professional photos of your own business make a big difference, so if you have them, or can take some, that is ideal. If not, I use high-quality images that fit your business.

Will I own the website?+

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site is yours after delivery. The first year of hosting and domain is included; after that, you renew them each year.

What if I need changes later?+

Because the site is built from your briefing, two rounds of changes are included to fine-tune it before launch. After that, small updates can be done on request or with an optional monthly care plan.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?+

Yes. I redesign it from scratch, keeping what already works for you.

Do you work with businesses outside Brazil?+

Yes. Everything is done online, in English or Spanish, wherever you are.
